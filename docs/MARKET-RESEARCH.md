# International market research checklist

Before launching a product in a new country, validate at least:

## Demand
- Search demand
- Competitor density
- Local product alternatives
- Seasonality
- Customer reviews and pain points

## Offer
- Localized pricing
- Currency expectations
- Payment methods
- Delivery promise
- Returns policy

## Acquisition
- Google Ads auction conditions
- Meta creative fit
- Search intent
- Localized landing page language
- Tracking quality

## Operations
- Supplier lead time
- Shipping cost
- Returns address
- Customer support coverage
- Chargeback risk

## Compliance
- Consumer rights
- Privacy requirements
- Product restrictions
- Tax and VAT obligations
- Customs and duties

This checklist is operational guidance, not legal or tax advice.
